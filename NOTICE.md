Hiring Signal Radar's original source code is licensed under the MIT License.

Job-posting metadata is collected from public employer-controlled Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby job boards. That metadata is factual source material and is not relicensed by this repository. Employers and platform providers retain any rights they may have in their names, marks, board content, and services.

The project stores compact structured metadata and direct source links. It does not store complete job descriptions, candidate information, resumes, application data, or authentication credentials. Source URLs are limited to HTTP(S), upgraded to HTTPS, and stripped of common tracking parameters.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any tracked employer or applicant-tracking-system provider. Operators are responsible for reviewing applicable source terms before changing the collector or adding a provider.
